# Korashi Books — Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What are your opening hours?

Korashi Books is open from Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The shop is closed on Fridays.

## 2. Where is Korashi Books located?

Korashi Books is a fictional independent bookshop located in Downtown Cairo, Egypt. The shop is near Talaat Harb Square.

## 3. Do you ship to Alexandria?

Yes. Korashi Books ships to Alexandria and other cities across Egypt. Orders are usually delivered within 2–4 business days, depending on the destination.

## 4. What is your return policy?

Customers can request a return within 14 days of receiving their order. Books must be unused and in their original condition. Customers should contact the shop before returning an item.

## 5. What payment methods do you accept?

Korashi Books accepts cash on delivery and online payment by Visa or Mastercard. Payment availability may depend on the delivery location.

## 6. Do you have books in English?

Yes. Korashi Books offers books in both Arabic and English. English titles include fiction, non-fiction, and educational books.

## 7. Can I request a special order?

Yes. Customers can request a special order for a book that is not currently available in the shop. Availability depends on the publisher or supplier. Customers should contact the shop with the book title and author.

## 8. How can I contact Korashi Books?

Customers can contact Korashi Books through the contact form on its website or by sending a message through its official social media pages. The shop does not publish personal phone numbers.

## 9. Do you sell textbooks?

Korashi Books carries a selection of educational and academic books. If a specific textbook is not available, customers can ask about a special order.

## 10. Do you offer discounts?

Korashi Books does not have a permanent discount policy. Any temporary offers or promotions will be announced on the shop's official social media pages.
